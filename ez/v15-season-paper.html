      How we grade

      1. Direction first. Did the model call up or down right?
      2. Then size. Among the models that got the direction right, the one closest to the real move wins the round.
      3. Nobody right, nobody wins. If every model gets the direction wrong, the round has no winner.
      4. Ties. Equal wins are split by who was closer on average.
