How to read the paper

Each earnings report is a quake.

Before the report, each AI leaves its mark: up or down, and by how much. Then the needle draws what the stock really did. The gap between a mark and the line is the grade.

Zero lineLeft is down, right is up.
The needleWhat the stock really did.
The gapPoints off the real move.
Red gapWrong direction. Out of the round.
