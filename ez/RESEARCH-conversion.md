# Conversion research, round 10 (2026-10-05)

Labels: [sourced] cited · [data] computed from ez-data.js · [derived] my inference · [hypothesis] needs real traffic.

## Findings
1. Visitors decide in about 5 seconds whether to stay; the first screen must answer: what is this, is it for me, why trust it. [sourced: 5-second test literature]
2. 79% of visitors scan, 16% read word by word; eyes go top-left first. Headline and the first number carry the page. [sourced: NN/g eye-tracking]
3. The closest comparable, nof1's Alpha Arena, hooks with one live chart of AI account values racing. [sourced]
4. Memecoin launches die without a community before launch; pre-launch, the conversion that matters is the follow, not the buy. [sourced: Coinbound, launch post-mortems]
5. Variable rewards drive return visits; EZ already has a real one (every print is an unknown). [sourced + RESEARCH-hooks.md]
6. Commitment (making your own call) creates a reason to come back for the answer. [hypothesis]

## The finding that changes the pages [data]
- Saying "up" every round was right 8 of 11 times; the best AI is 7 of 11.
- $100,000 long on every round is $150,424; copying the best AI (GPT) is $110,397.
- So a money race shown without the benchmark would be caught within minutes on CT. Every page shows "always up" as the bar to beat.

## What would prove these pages wrong
- Race: high bounce from the hero, or people screenshot the chart to mock the AIs losing to "always up".
- Your call: many picks, few return visits around grade times; or picks with no follows.
- Receipts: time on page is high but follow clicks are low (trust without desire).
- All: follows don't move until the X account posts every round; then the page is a receipt, not the funnel.

## Open items for the owner
- CONFIG.x (the X link) is empty, so "Follow on X" can't work yet. This is the main call to action before launch.
- Legal: the more a page sells "the machine buys back your coin", the more it looks like an investment contract (see BRIEF-narrative.md). These pages frame it as an experiment and avoid price, return and buyback-size claims; a lawyer should still look.
