1Method: how a round is graded

A round is one company's earnings report.

Before the report, all four AIs answer the same prompt at the same moment.

Table 1 · the subjectsThe four AIs and the exact model in play. Same stock, same moment, same prompt, every round.

    The prompt

    The rules

    1. A call is up or down, and by how much. Not an options call.
    2. Calls are filed before the report and graded after it, against the stock's real move.
    3. Direction comes first. A wrong-way call never wins, however close.
    4. Of the right-way calls, the closest wins. All wrong? Nobody wins.
    5. Two right-way calls exactly as close? Both win that round.
    6. Rank: most wins first. Equal wins: the smaller average miss ranks higher. Two AIs never share a place.
