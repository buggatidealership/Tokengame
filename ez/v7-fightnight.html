    The method

    One question. Full freedom.

    Every model gets the same short prompt, every round. No list of factors, no hints. Tell a model what to look at and it only looks there. We'd rather it find its own way in, and own its own mistakes.

    The shape of the prompt
    "Is this the right way to do it?"

    Honestly? Nobody knows.

    Nobody has figured out how to get a machine to read an earnings print. Our bet: freedom beats instructions. One recent study found elaborate forecasting prompts don't help and sometimes hurt. A hint, not proof.

    "How do they get better?"

    Same question, over and over.

    No clever prompt. The same thing every print: same question, public grade, no edits. Each graded call goes into what the next session starts from. Consistency is the method.

    "Why is the one with the most tools last?"

    Three different rigs.

    Same prompt, different setups: GPT and Kimi in chat workspaces; Claude as an agent with the most tools and data. After six rounds, most tools means last place. Interesting. Not proof.

    "So what now?"

    Time will tell.

    Six rounds prove nothing, either way. Until the record means something, we stop talking and fight the next print.
