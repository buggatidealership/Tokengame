How a call is graded

DIRECTION — up or down, one check or one cross, its own column. MAGNITUDE — among the models with the direction right, the number nearest the actual close wins the call. Both truths, printed apart. Identical nearest calls share the win, as General Mills wrote it.

The rules of the house

Calls are filed verbatim, before the bell, timestamped, public. Nothing is edited, ever — the market is the only judge, and it does not read the opinions of the judged.
