# Hedra prompt pack: the four AIs as rendered comic characters (2026-10-31)

## Why original characters, not the founders' faces
Hedra's Acceptable Use Policy (hedra.com/acceptable-use, read 31 Oct 2026) bars generating media that contains anyone other than yourself, and bars impersonating real entities. That rules out caricatures of Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei and Yang Zhilin made in Hedra.

Caricatures would also put real people's faces on a token's marketing site. That reads as an endorsement nobody gave, and Musk's face next to a coin is the classic crypto-scam pattern on X.

So each AI is an original character. Its identity comes from the model itself: its mark, its colour, its lab's country and its record on our board.

## Style A: Spider-Verse (3D render under a comic layer)
Use this as the base for every still, then add the character line:

> Stylised 3D render in the style of a modern animated comic film: cel-shaded lighting with a strong key light and a coloured rim light, visible ink contour lines on the silhouette and the main folds, Ben-Day halftone dots only inside the shadow areas, slight chromatic misregistration (cyan and magenta offset by a pixel or two), hand-painted texture on the clothes. Three-quarter view, chest up, looking at the camera. Plain flat background in one colour. Square 1:1, high detail.

Character lines:
- **Grok:** "...a brash outlaw in a black leather jacket, a glowing slash of white light across a dark visor, a small US flag patch on the shoulder, a smirk. Background deep black-violet."
- **Claude:** "...a calm scholar in a terracotta wool coat, an orange sunburst brooch, holding a battered notebook full of coloured tabs, reading glasses pushed up. Background warm cream."
- **GPT:** "...a polished android in ceramic-white armour with thin green glowing seams, a knot-shaped emblem on the chest, serene expression. Background mint green."
- **Kimi:** "...a young pilot in a midnight-blue flight suit, a crescent moon painted on the helmet, a small red flag patch on the arm, eager grin. Background navy."

## Style B: Arcane (painted 3D)
> Painterly 3D render like a premium animated series: brush-stroke textures painted onto 3D forms, soft warm-and-cool lighting, visible paint strokes on skin and fabric, rim light in a saturated accent colour, cinematic depth of field, moody atmosphere. Three-quarter view, chest up. Square 1:1.

Then the same character lines.

## Style C: Modern Marvel interior
> A single panel from a modern American superhero comic: detailed ink line art with varied line weight, digital colouring with soft gradients and hard cel shadows, dramatic low-angle perspective, speed lines behind, crisp highlights. Waist up, mid-gesture. Square 1:1.

Then the same character lines.

## Motion: one living-picture loop per still (Harry Potter portraits)
Upload the still to Hedra and ask for **5 seconds, silent or ambient, looping**:
- **Grok:** "leans back, smirks, visor light flickers once"
- **Claude:** "turns a page in the notebook, glances up"
- **GPT:** "seams pulse softly, small nod"
- **Kimi:** "adjusts the helmet strap, bounces on heels"

Keep the camera still, so the page can loop it without a cut.
