# H47 · bronze: the season as a Bronze Age event series

## Take
American Bronze Age comic book: the season is an event series, the Overview is the latest issue, the Leaderboard is the back of the book.

- **The issue:**
  - Issue number = graded rounds (`ROUNDS.length`).
  - Phone reads page by page.
  - Desktop reads as facing pages: the cover beside page 1, the big moments as double-page spreads.
- **The art:** painted in Hedra, refs being the four founder stills, in one recipe: realistic pencils, brush ink with spotted blacks, four-colour separations, Ben-Day tints, yellowed newsprint.
- **The surfaces:** every page surface is colour multiplied into a painted newsprint scan, with a page light and dot screens on the tints.

## How the addendum's story is told
### Overview, in order
1. **Cover:** corner box (EZ. logo, No. 84, month, the four model marks), extruded "EARNINGS ZEASON" masthead, "Starring GPT · Claude · Grok · Kimi". One blurb: "Which AI can you trust on earnings? We track every trade so you don't have to!" Four silhouettes under the closing bell keep the cast's faces for later.
2. **Page 1, splash** ("We track the AIs so you don't have to!"):
   - four hands slam four slips; each sleeve is a founder's;
   - caption: each AI goes long or short before earnings;
   - caption: the close grades every trade;
   - the verdict panel (top model by season P&L): portrait with a "Golden ticket" tag, "Grok leads", "Right on 49 of 76 trades (64.5%)", +$162,950, and a Wilson 95% band (53–74%) captioned "luck still counts";
   - credits box (traders / stakes / grader);
   - the indicia carries the small print once: simulated, $100K a trade, before fees; not financial advice; trading can lose money; the pool can be wiped out; Hyperliquid markets as of Nov 3; not available in every country.
3. **Page 2, "Grok's next trades"** (the old "Next issue!" device, moved to the front):
   - one panel per company with trades in, in the company's brand colour, with logo and name (ticker);
   - the top model's face says LONG or SHORT in a balloon; the other three are chips;
   - foot strip: "Follow it on Hyperliquid: xyz:AMD · $17.9M open interest", or "thin market" under $10M, or "No Hyperliquid market";
   - then the not-yet-traded companies: "They drop before the earnings."
4. **The receipts:** one round step by step, the newest graded round with a film (R80 XOM):
   - balloons;
   - the round's poster, the company's logo, then "ZIP! +2.17%";
   - W/L stamps with dollars, "CLOSEST!" on the winner;
   - "...and round 80 goes on the record."
5. **The story so far:** every round newest first.
   - The poster is the panel.
   - The move is a sound effect breaking the corner, sized by the move.
   - The caption gives name (ticker) and the closest trade ("GPT & Claude tie: long +2%", or "All wrong. Nobody wins.").
   - "Show all 84 rounds".
6. **Meet the traders:** the cast as wrapper.
   - Each card: name, flag patch, season P&L, W-L.
   - A computed personality line, e.g. "Goes short on 88% of its trades, and expects the smallest moves."
   - The latest trade.
7. **Smart or lucky?:**
   - a painted crowd shouts "Are they any good... or just lucky?!*";
   - an inked hit-rate chart ((right+1)/(trades+2)) and a P&L chart;
   - editor's footnote: "*84 rounds is too few to tell skill from luck... Ed."
8. **The bet**, the climactic splash:
   - "Bet #1: NVIDIA · At the close · Nov 18";
   - a pool of coins with "The pool" engraved on its plaque, and the caption "69% of $EZ creator fees fill the pool" (SIM.poolShare);
   - the golden ticket in a green beam: "Its trade decides long or short. Now: Grok.";
   - the countdown;
   - RIGHT and WRONG as two crops of the same painting: the glowing pool, and the cracked rim spilling coins into the pit;
   - "NVIDIA is the first bet, not the last."
9. **The Mailbag:** follow on Hyperliquid, real money, who decides, why Grok has fewer trades (it joined at round 9; computed).

### Leaderboard
- Trade dress, then "Who to follow now".
- Painted golden ticket with the holder lettered on it, plus the freeze rule; the small print once.
- The control is a mail-order coupon of radio buttons ticked in red pen: rank by P&L or hit rate; window Season, 7 days or 24 hours.
- The verdict for that window and rank:
  - leader, W of N, hit rate, the window's P&L, the range band;
  - ties named ("Tied with GPT on hit rate; ahead on P&L");
  - an empty 24-hour window says so with the last close and never falls back.
- Grok's next trades, the same panels as the Overview.
- The receipts:
  - the table: P&L, Last 5 oldest → newest with the newest ringed, W-L, hit rate, and P&L at the latest close (named "Tue Nov 3 close"), the leader's row in gold ink with no word;
  - the window's P&L chart (the hit-rate chart when ranking by hit rate);
  - by sector, won/made, the best in each row circled in red grease pencil;
  - "Only companies with a Hyperliquid market today", with its caveat.
- Files, one typed page per model: mugshot, model and lab, season P&L, W-L, hit rate, Last 5, Biggest W and Biggest L (poster, company (ticker), round, side, move, amount), best sector.

## Research used
- **Colour:** CMY at 25/50/100% on newsprint gives about 64 colours, with Ben-Day tints ([Ben Day process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Day_process); [Marvel Codex, Comic Book Coloring](https://marvelcodex.com/comic_book_coloring)).
- **Cover:** the corner box's "magic inverted L", and one blurb carrying the conflict ([Tom Brevoort, Covers part four](https://tombrevoort.com/2020/02/08/blah-blah-blog-covers-part-four/); [GoCollect, newsstand corner art](https://gocollect.com/blog/unappreciated-art-newsstand-comic-books-corner-art)).
- **Lettering:** caption types, asterisk to editor's note, tails aimed at the mouth, bursts for shouts, hand-lettered or hollow sound effects, double dashes ([Blambot, Comic Book Grammar & Tradition](https://blambot.com/articles_grammar.shtml); [Speech balloon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_balloon)).
- **Era:** about 1970–85, realism as the norm ([Bronze Age of Comic Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age_of_Comic_Books)).
- **Letters column:** run by the editor, doubling as a house ad ([Comic book letter column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_book_letter_column)).

No publisher logo, code seal, title or character from any property was used.

## Checks (Playwright, getBoundingClientRect)
- **Sideways scroll:** scrollWidth equals the viewport on both pages at 320, 390 and 1280. A 344px overflow at 320 from the last-close strip was fixed.
- **Fonts:** exactly three rendered, BZH (Bowlby One SC, the one heading face), BZL (Kalam 700), BZT (Courier Prime).
- **Words:** none of the banned words (call, prediction, coin flip, pit wall, pot, volume, proven, beats the market, getting smarter). No emoji.
- **Gap list 17, alignment:** Leaderboard W-L, hit rate and P&L labels sit 0.00px off their figures at 320, 390 and 1280; the sector columns share one grid.
- **Gap list 17 and lesson 119, repeated geometry:**

  | Cell | 320 | 390 | 1280 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Cast cards (width) | 142 | 177 | 290 |
  | Cast portraits (height) | 165.6 | 207.6 | 343.2 |
  | Trade tiers | 67×98.3 | 84.5×123.9 | 140×256.7 |
  | Next-trade faces | 86×74 | 86×74 | 104×86 |
  | File mugshots | 120×156 | 150×196 | 249×324 |

  Record rows are equal in height.
- **Dead space:** each page ends 20px after its content (the folio slot) at 320 and 390, and 20–25px at 1280 (the cover 10px). Two desktop spreads were rebalanced from 91 and 62px. The desktop next-trades column is filled with the not-yet-in companies.
- **Clipping:** no clipped text. The shout burst is drawn 4px outside its box on purpose.
- **Gap list 19, surfaces:** every ground is colour × painted newsprint with light and dot screens; the W/L boxes and countdown dies are textured too.
- **Motion:** none. The countdown text refreshes every 30 s; reduced motion turns off all animation.
- **Generated art:** no words or numbers in it. The exceptions are the posters (the rounds' official art) and the "$" on GPT's coin, which is in the reference.
