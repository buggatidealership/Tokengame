# Round H8 · wow: capabilities the owner has not asked for

Rough mockups. Each is mounted on the real X3+ page (`v47-live-x3plus.html`, served locally) right after the cover, and reads the page's own data (ROUNDS, UPCOMING, `__x2pPnl()`), except W5, which works on the real Meet the models cards. Painted plates are low-res Hedra roughs (gpt-image-2, 1K medium); every word and number is page text set on a painted surface (the vault's brass plaque, the exchange's clock face and gallery rail, the betting slips, the cards). Motion is transforms and opacity only; nothing moves with reduced motion.

Boards: `ez/x2p-x3p-h8-wow-1.png` to `-6.png`. Videos: `ez/x2p-x3p-h8-wow-<n>.mp4` (2 and 6 also have a `-desktop.mp4`; 6 has sound).

## The two that would sit near the top: first-screen cost

Measured on a 4x slower CPU at 390x844, the scene mounted right after the cover, medians of 3 page loads (`perf.js`): main-thread time to mount, while sitting 6 s on the scene, and during a 2 s scroll through it.

| Scene | Mount (s) | Sitting 6 s (s) | Scrolling 2 s (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| none (the page alone) | 1.67 | 4.48 | 3.59 |
| 2 the floor | 2.99 | 4.58 | 3.41 |
| 4 depth | 0.43 | 4.43 | 4.37 |
| 1 the vault | 2.63 | 4.94 | 3.49 |

Read with care: the page itself keeps the main thread busy about 75% of the time on this throttled CPU, so differences under about half a second are noise ("mount" is the window until the scene's images are decoded, which also catches the page's own work, so depth's lower figure only means its two images decoded sooner). What stands out: the floor costs about 1.3 s more to mount (three plates decoded at once; a real build loads only the current phase's plate and the rest on idle); the vault's slow plaque glint keeps about 0.5 s of work in 6 s while on screen (a transform animation, so it is compositing, but it still costs on a slow phone; stop it once the plaque has been tapped); depth's scroll listener adds about 0.8 s over a 2 s scroll (two transforms written per scroll event; a scroll-driven CSS animation would move this off the main thread where supported). None of it touches the cover's lift, since every scene mounts below the cover.
