# H29 · The Overview as one piece (the lead's spec; owner, 2026-11-02: "what would you do if you were in charge... reduce the amount of noise, but keep the aesthetics beautiful and a bit Harry Potter like")

The goal: a newcomer scrolls the X2+ Overview on a phone and, without effort, learns what this is, how it works, who is playing, the record, whether they are good, what is at stake, and to follow on X. One thing to look at per screen. Beautiful and a little magical: the pictures are alive the way portraits and newspaper photos are alive in Harry Potter, quietly, and only the one you are looking at.

Evidence: `h/flowaudit/REPORT.md` (live page, 2 Nov): 71–90 words on 6 of 9 phone screens, up to 5 movers, 4–6 typefaces per screen, 7 narrator styles, gold meaning 7 things, "call/trade/prediction" mixed, "win" with two meanings, three disagreeing "next"s, the page ending on an inert Get $EZ.

## The layers (each one a rule)
1. **Sequence (story).** headline → How a round works → Meet the models → Every round so far → Smart or lucky → the bet → Follow on X (the end) → a slim footer. Each section's last thing sets up the next one's first. The 1:20 film ("The whole game") moves into the headline section as a "Watch the 1:20 film" on the hero, so frame 4 (the board) hands straight to Meet the models.
2. **One focal point per phone screen.** One thing is clearly the biggest/brightest; everything else is quieter. About 40 words per phone screen at most; one narrator line per section.
3. **The gutter between sections** is one device everywhere: a wide torn-paper edge (comic book), dark paper, never white. Same height and look at every joint. It replaces today's gold strips and empty gaps.
4. **Narrator voice:** one yellow caption box (the How a round works C3 box: Bangers on #ffd21f-ish yellow, ink border, hard shadow, red number tab) carries each section's one explaining line (≤ 14 words). No other explaining styles.
5. **Typography: three faces.** Heading = Bangers (the "Bet #1: NVIDIA" lettering), numbers = Anton, text = Shantell Sans. Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Kanit, Saira fold into these. Phone scale (390): headline 40, section head 34, narrator 20, body 15, big number 28, table figure 16, label 12; nothing under 12px font-size on phones.
6. **Colour means one thing each.** Ground: the logo black #04070e and panels #0c0f17; text cream #f8f0d9; gold = headings and #1 only; green #03c870 = right / profit only; red #ff3a31 = wrong / loss only; model colours never green, red or gold (Grok's chart line is not the heading gold); no white or pale grounds anywhere (owner: "the only thing I can't stand").
7. **Geometry: one shape per section.** Repeated items share one size and shape (How a round works: four rows, each picture square, the caption box the same square; Every round so far: one tile shape; Meet the models: four equal chips).
8. **Motion: one mover per screen, only if it carries a fact.** The living-picture rule: the founder portrait of the chip nearest the screen centre plays, the others hold still like paintings; the latest round's picture plays its film muted when it is the focus; the countdown ticks. No pulsing dots, scan lines, die pulses, pointing hints or crawls. Still with reduced motion.
9. **Words.** "trade" for what an AI does each round; "W-L" = right or wrong direction; a round's winner is "Closest: Grok" (never "Grok wins"); the bet's "#1" says "#1 on the board"; one "next" source (the next close's companies and time) everywhere.
10. **Interaction.** Nothing looks tappable unless it is; no knobs; Get $EZ waits for launch (off the Overview until then); the page ends on one Follow on X block.

## More layers (owner, 2026-11-02: "what other rules... are a must that I didn't even ask"; the goal is follows on X and people buying the coin; the first audience is the memecoin trenches)
11. **Audience: the trenches.** They arrive on a phone from an X post or a Telegram link, inside the app's browser, sound off, thumb scrolling, and decide in seconds whether this is a project or a rug. Design for the first 3 seconds (what is it, is it alive) and the 2-minute check they run on every coin (website, X, the team's claims, the contract address).
12. **Trust (the 2-minute check).** Every claim is checkable: each trade posted on X before the report, graded at the close, the record public. Show the proof where they look: the X link with its follower count is not ours to show, but "every trade posted on X before the report" is; the contract address slot ("CA drops at launch", later a copy button with the full address, never shortened in the middle); no promises of returns; risk said once, plainly ("the pool can be wiped out").
13. **Conversion: two actions, one primary at a time.** Before launch: Follow on X is primary everywhere; Buy is a quiet "Launching on pump.fun soon". After launch: a slim sticky bar on phones (Follow · Buy $EZ, CA copy) that appears after the first screen and never covers a number; the end of the page repeats the primary action. Every section's last beat can lead to the follow ("Follow for the next round").
14. **Liveness (a reason to come back).** The page shows what is happening now: the next close and the companies in it, the latest round as the newest thing. Coming back = following. Nothing on the page may look stale (old dates, a "next" that has passed).
15. **Share and screenshot.** Every section reads as a screenshot on its own (a trader posts the P&L board or a round card); round links have their own picture (/r/n, live). Numbers formatted like a P&L card (+$148,480, green/red, no clutter).
16. **Voice.** Trench-native but not cringe: short, dry, confident; the models' quotes carry the humour ("Shorts almost everything. Somehow it's working."); no hype words (moon, 100x, guaranteed), no emoji.
17. **Speed and the in-app browser.** First screen visible fast on a mid phone over mobile data; memory budget (no black tiles at 40 MB GPU); muted autoplay only; no layout jumps; works in X's and Telegram's in-app browsers.
18. **Access.** Contrast 4.5:1 for text; colour never the only carrier (patterns, W/L letters); reduced motion respected; tap targets 44px.

## Art direction (one style for the whole Overview)
The world: a night trading floor built of circuit boards, painted (the NVIDIA bet chip, `media/bet-chip-h2.jpg`, is the reference): black void, one warm key light, green and red rim lights, gold only for #1 and headings. Every section is an object in that world (the headline chip, the four round panels, the four model chips on one board, the latest front page, the etched charts board, the bet chip, the X slab at the end), joined by the same torn-paper gutters, lettered in the same comic hand (Bangers heads, yellow narrator boxes). Living pictures, one at a time. No white or pale ground.
