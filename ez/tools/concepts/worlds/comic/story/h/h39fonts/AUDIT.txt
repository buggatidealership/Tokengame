H39 ONE FONT SYSTEM ON THE OVERVIEW (X2+, ez/v46-live-x2plus.html), 2026-11-03
Owner: "Different fonts on the card on Bet#1. Make sure all fonts are the same across each beat or module or header."

HOW IT WAS MEASURED
Every visible text node of every Overview section (main > section: top, Every round so far, Meet the models,
Smart or lucky, the bet + Season 2, Up next) was collected in Chromium with the site's real Google fonts.
For each one: computed family, size, weight, case, letter-spacing, colour, and the faces that actually
render the glyphs (CDP CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode). Hidden, clipped and off-screen text was left out.
"Before" = the same build with x2p_more/x2p_sol/x2p_top taken back to commit 3c8114eb (the state before this
pass), so other builders' work (the vitrine, the leaderboard) is identical on both sides.
Scripts: scratchpad h39fonts/audit.js, roles.js, check.js, shot.js, board.py.

THE SYSTEM AFTER (three faces only)
  Bangers        lettering: section heading (gold), sub-heading (cream), every caption box and its kicker,
                 the How a round works step captions, buttons and the rail switch.
  Anton          numbers, and every label printed on an object: tabs on an edge, die and chip silkscreen,
                 the Mag 7 plate, a slab's label, table titles, model names in a table, the lit RIGHT / WRONG,
                 the step-number tabs, words lettered inside a painting (LONG/SHORT on the dies, WINNER, MON).
  Shantell Sans  body lines (800): the lines under RIGHT / WRONG and the scam line; and the lower-case "s"
                 of AIs inside Bangers capitals.
Faces rendered on the Overview, glyph runs: before Bangers 46, Anton 191, Shantell 49, DejaVu Sans 1,
Liberation Sans 2 (390px); after Bangers 48, Anton 223, Shantell 14, nothing else (390 and 1280).

DECISIONS
- The bet card's caption is now the page's caption box (cream #f8f0d9, 2.5px ink border, 3px 4px ink shadow,
  -.8deg, Bangers capitals, .015em, line-height 1.02, the same size as Smart or lucky / Up next / Season 2).
  Words unchanged; "69%" still from SIM.poolShare. The box hugs its words and still sits on the seam
  (seam() re-measures it): at 390 it is 74px tall (was 98) and now clears the DAYS/HRS labels by 13px (was 1px).
- "69%" inside the caption is lettered in Bangers like the rest of the box, as every other caption box letters
  its numbers ("1 to 10", "$100K", "Season 1"). "Numbers: Anton" is applied to figures, not words of a caption.
- Underlines dropped: no other caption box underlines anything (Smart or lucky, Up next, Season 2, Every round,
  Meet the models). "the whole pool" and "#1 AI's" now read like the rest of the line.
- The "s" of AIs stays lower case in Shantell 800 .86em in every caption box. In the gold section heading
  ("Four AIs call earnings.") it is Bangers at .8em (the heading's own convention); left as is.
- RIGHT / WRONG: the words are lit labels on the painting (Anton capitals, kept); the lines under them
  ("Profits buy back $EZ.", "The pool shrinks or can be wiped out.") are body text, now the same style as the
  page's other body line (the scam line under Up next).
- Caption box sizes were already one at 390 (17px) but not on desktop: Meet the models and Smart or lucky
  26px, the others 24px. Now 24px from 1000px everywhere (15-17px below 760, 17-26px between).
- Tabs: "At the close · Nov 18" was Shantell 800, "Next earnings season" Anton; both Anton now, one size.
- "Every other round · newest first" (the old slab box sub-heading) is set as a sub-heading with Season 2's
  lettering, the dot dropped where it breaks to two lines. The vitrine (x2p_vit) now hides that block, so it is
  only the fallback; the m7 slab-label rules (Anton, sizes scaled .82 for Anton's taller capitals) are the
  same story: in place for the fallback, not visible today.
- Fallback glyphs gone: the switch arrow (▸/◂ fell back to DejaVu Sans) and the schedule button's arrow (→ fell
  back to Liberation Sans) are drawn with CSS clip-paths in the text colour.
