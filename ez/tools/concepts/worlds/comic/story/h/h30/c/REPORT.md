## What could make it fail
- Trustworthy but forgettable: nothing ownable; the film poster is the only art above the fold.
- Word budget over in four frames.
- Grok's lead is partly from fewer trades (72 vs 80); the race doesn't say so.
- Smart or lucky: one point clear, four separate tests, caveat unstated.
- 7-day figure is volatile; tape columns 7.8px on a phone; hollow L slash thin (1.1px).
- Relative times don't refresh; holidays not handled. Safari, slow phone, screen reader not checked.
