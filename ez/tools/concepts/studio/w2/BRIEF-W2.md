# W2 · Matchday: brief and concept (stages 1 and 2)

**Brief (one line):** a visitor must see, in one screen, that four AIs trade every earnings report like teams on matchday (what is live now, what kicks off tonight in their own time, how last night finished, who tops the table) and then tap a fixture, a result or the table.

**Concept (one sentence):** every earnings report is a fixture between LONG and SHORT, the four AIs are the teams that line up on either side, the report is kickoff and the next market close is the final whistle, so the site reads like a live sports app: Today, Fixtures, Results, Table, team pages and a match centre whose timeline is the night's P&L.

Style frame: the Today screen at 390px (scorebug, the live fixture, tonight's fixtures, the table strip, last night's final scores). Checked as a screenshot before any motion was built.
