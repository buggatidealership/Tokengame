# Release video, creative brief (draft 1, 2026-11-09)

From the owner's answers in the discovery session, 2026-11-09.

## The one thing to remember
"I can follow what the AIs are calling, and I can check how often they've been right."
Put a number on trust: we give you the data, you make the call.

## Audience
Mainly crypto degens trading with leverage on Hyperliquid; AI enthusiasts second.
Their doubt: "you can't trust an AI." Our answer is the number, not a promise.

## Proof (only screens that exist on earningszn.xyz)
- Leaderboard: P&L, W-L, hit rate per AI (the number on trust).
- Upcoming: Live now card; Next trades with "Trades drop by <time>"; Last matchday with a row opened (each AI's side, right or wrong).
- An AI profile: every trade it has made.

## Reference
Fomo's own app clips (the owner's uploads `fomo-profile-720.mp4`, `fomo-leaderboard-720.mp4`): the real interface on screen,
zoom-ins, pieces lifting out of the screen, fast cuts. Our closest so far: `ez/x2p-reveal-v5-A-m1-trailer.mp4`
(the owner liked its music and its pace; not the 3-2-1 circles or the lock). V2 Broadcast is the resolution video, not this.

## Call to action
One: the next trade drops at <day, time>. Follow @earningszn.

## Sound
Music only. No voice.

## Success
Reach, profile visits, and the share of profile visits that turn into follows.

## Words
- Never promise returns or "better bets". The site shows hit rates near a coin flip (Claude 56/99, Grok 56/91, GPT 52/99, Kimi 46/99 on 2026-11-09): show the number, never a verdict on it.
- "Never late to a trade" only once the prediction harness posts every trade on time (the owner's commitment, not built yet).

## Defaults until the owner says otherwise
- 4:5, 1080x1350, 20 to 25 s, readable with the sound off, for a pinned post on X.
- Recorded from the live site on a fixed clock with real data.
