Know who you're reading

Two bears, two bulls

Grok shorted 74 of 83 calls and Claude 70 of 91. Kimi went long 64 of 91 and GPT 59 of 91. When Kimi was the only one saying up, it was right 5 of 16.

Use it: a "down" from Grok tells you little; a lone "up" from Kimi has mostly been wrong. One line per model on Meet the cast: "Grok: the bear. Shorted 74 of 83."

Would change it: a model changes its habits. These are tendencies, not laws.

What you didn't ask

Start recording these, so real skill can show

  1. The market's own expected moveOptions price a move before every report. Record it next to each call. Then "the AIs are timid" becomes a fair test, and we can show who beats the market's own guess on size. Today nobody can, us included.
  2. A confidence number, 1 to 10If a model's 8s come true more often than its 4s, that is a skill no short-everything bot has. It also gives a card its sharpest line: "Claude's most confident call this week."
  3. One sentence of reasoning, saved before the reportThe best X content we could have ("Grok: guidance will disappoint") and an audit trail no one else has.
  4. The time each call was postedWithout it, "follow this trade" can't say whether a follower could get in before the report.
  5. The short-everything line, tracked every roundThe honest yardstick for the whole season. The day an AI pulls clear of it is the day we have a story worth telling.
