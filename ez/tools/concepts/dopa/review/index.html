What "polished" is measured as

Round 4 is built against these. Round 2 was made before you said it and fails the first two.

  • It rolls out"roll it out step by step, so somebody understands what they are looking at"Each scene opens with a plain line alone for 0.7 s. Things arrive one at a time, 0.7 s apart, and stay. The scene holds 1.6 s after the last one. Words come before numbers; numbers are rounded.
  • Two data points a scene"each screen is to show 2 data points max, and the 2 data points need to correlate"At most two numbers or verdicts at full contrast, about the same subject. A table arrives one row a scene.
  • Not crowded, not empty"not having too much dead space... not too crowded"At most 12 words a scene. Content centred, 35 to 70% of the frame's height, no empty band over 30%.
  • Glass"this glassy aspect with Apple"One frosted sheet a scene over slow brand-colour light, bright only on its top edge. On take K, the NVIDIA finale beats and Season 2 mockup A.
  • Calm motion"max animation... cleaner"Nothing ever frozen, at most two things moving at once, every arrival under half a second.
