# Meet the models: art kit for the painter (2026-10-31)

The site sets every word and number in code; the art carries **no text, no numbers, no logos baked in**. The style is the one the owner picked (`tools/concepts/worlds/comic/story/gr/ref-dario-A-3dfirst.png`): 3D-modelled forms and light, then a comic finish (ink lines, halftone dots only in the shadows), rich colour, red and green key lights.

## Style line (start every prompt with it)
> Stylised 3D render finished as a comic book page: modelled faces and materials, hard red and green key lights with a warm practical light, ink contour lines on silhouettes and main folds, halftone dots only inside the shadow areas, slight colour misregistration, painted texture. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

## Layers to make (PNG, as large as the tool gives)
1. **Backdrop, one plate:** "...an empty backstage corridor before a fight, four dressing-room stations side by side, mirrors with vanity bulbs, road cases, hanging robes, haze in the air, deep shadows, wide 21:9, nothing in the foreground." (Desktop: the four cards sit inside this one scene.)
2. **Backdrop, phone:** the same corridor as one station, 4:5.
3. **The four fighters, each on a plain flat green background (so I can cut them out), waist up, three-quarter view, same light and scale:** the owner's green room caricatures (Grok, GPT, Claude, Kimi), each in a robe in its model's colour (Grok black and gold, GPT green, Claude orange, Kimi blue).
4. **Props, each alone on flat green:** a blank weathered sign plate with two stars (no words); a gold-leaf golden ticket (no words); a lit vanity bulb and a dead one; a strip of gaffer tape.
5. **Motion (Hedra):** each fighter's still as a 4 s silent loop, camera locked: breathing, a glance, a small gesture. Same first and last frame.

## What I do with them
Cut the layers out, stack them with depth (the backdrop drifts slower than the fighters as you scroll), let the bulbs flicker and the light breathe, play the loops only on screen, and set the name, P&L, record and last five in the site's lettering on the sign plate, tape and ticket. One module first; if it works, the same kit pattern for every section.
