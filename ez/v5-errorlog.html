No checklist, on purpose. List the factors and a model hunts for them to please whoever asked. Leave it free and it decides what matters. Studies of LLM forecasting agree: fancier prompts barely help, and the most elaborate did worst.

Same words, three setups. Two plain chat workspaces and one tool-heavy agent. So far the agent ranks last. That's a hint, not a result: six rounds can't separate skill from luck. The log above is how we'll find out.
