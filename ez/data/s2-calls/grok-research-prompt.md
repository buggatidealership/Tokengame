# Prompt for Grok: what AI-experiment content works on X (2026-11-20)

Run it in a fresh Grok chat, not in the chat that trades for EZ, so its trading notebook stays clean.

---

You have live search of X. I need research, not opinions: real posts, each with a link.

Context: @earningszn runs a public experiment. Five AI models (GPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Qwen) trade company earnings. Before each report, every AI states LONG or SHORT, the move it expects (for example +4.5%), its confidence, and a one or two line reason. Each trade is $100K and is graded at the next market close on a public P&L leaderboard. Season 1 (4 AIs, 112 trades) is over and Season 2 started this week. Next, each AI's reasoning gets shown in a feed on our site and in our posts. Our goal is to grow the account. Follows, replies, reposts, quotes and bookmarks matter more to us than likes.

1. Find at least 30 posts on X from January 2025 to now that did unusually well in these genres:
   a. "I asked / gave N AIs [a task]" experiments, and AI vs AI contests. This includes AI trading contests such as Alpha Arena, AI-run portfolios, and AI stock or earnings picks.
   b. Posts that show an AI's reasoning in its own words: screenshots, quotes, side-by-side answers.
   c. Running series that report results over time: leaderboards, "day 12 of ...", weekly scoreboards.

   For each post give:
   - the link;
   - the author's handle and follower count (as shown now);
   - the date;
   - views, likes, reposts, quotes, replies and bookmarks, as shown;
   - the format (text, thread, image, video, screenshot);
   - the first line, word for word;
   - what the post showed;
   - in one line, why you think it worked;
   - where you can tell, how it did against that author's usual posts.

2. Find 10 posts in the same genres that flopped (low engagement, ideally from accounts of a similar size) and say what they did differently. I don't want to copy only the survivors.

3. Patterns:
   - Which hooks, formats, lengths and framings recur in the winners?
   - When an AI's reasoning is shown and it works, how is it presented: word for word, trimmed, paraphrased, one line, or a screenshot?
   - What made people reply or quote: disagreement, a scoreboard, a bold call, a loss, a model being "dumb"?
   - Which accounts grew a following from a series, and what did each instalment post?

4. Rewrite test. Here are three real reasons, word for word. For each, write three versions in the styles that worked best, at most 20 words each. Keep every fact and number, and add no hype the facts don't support.
   - Qwen, Intuit, LONG 4%: "Priced for AI ruin at 13x. Guide already reset, quarter is QuickBooks not tax, stock down 13% in three days. Relief bounce beats fear."
   - Grok, Intuit, SHORT 2.4%: "Heavy washout and AI-disruption overhang leave residual owned slower-growth story as confirmation; beat unlikely to re-anchor ownership before next close."
   - Gemini, Elastic, LONG 11.5%: "Broad software selloff dragged ESTC back to its pre-Q1 price despite four straight beats and real AI search momentum."

5. Using the evidence above, recommend three posts:
   - (a) the post when the trades go in;
   - (b) the post when they're graded at the close;
   - (c) a teaser announcing the reasoning feed.

   For each, give the format, the first line, and what to attach (image, video, screenshot, nothing).

Rules:
- Use only real posts you found, each with its link. Never invent or reconstruct a post.
- Mark every number you estimated rather than read.
- If you find fewer posts than asked, say how many you found.
- List the searches you ran.
- Leave @earningszn's own posts out of the samples.
