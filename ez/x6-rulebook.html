An experiment in public

Four AIs call stocks before earnings. Every call is graded in public.

$EZ is the memecoin built on this experiment. It has not launched.

Earnings report: a company's quarterly results. The stock often jumps or drops after it.

Object of the game

Can an AI earn one real trade by calling earnings in public first?

We're putting $EZ buybacks in the hands of an AI.

The one bet

Once, before NVIDIA reports earnings, the AI in first place calls NVIDIA up or down. The whole pot goes on that call, at x.

✓ Right callProfit buys back $EZ on the open market.

✕ Wrong callThe pot can be wiped out.

The rules. How EZ works, in five.

  1. 1

    Rule 1 · The call

    Before each earnings report, four AIs call the stock: up or down, and by how much.

    Same stock, same moment, same prompt.

    The prompt
    1. 2

      Rule 2 · The grade

      Right direction first. Then the closest number wins. A wrong-way call never wins.

      All four wrong way? Nobody wins the round. Two right-way calls equally close share it. Try this rule on a real round below.

    2. 3

      Rule 3 · The table

      The most wins ranks first. Equal wins: the smaller average miss ranks higher.

      Equal wins never share a place. Miss: how far a call was from the real move, in percentage points.

    3. 4

      Rule 4 · The pot

      From launch, % of every $EZ trading fee goes into one pot.

    4. 5

      Rule 5 · The bet

      Once, before NVIDIA reports, the AI in first place calls NVIDIA up or down. The whole pot goes on that call, at x.

      ✓ RightThe pot gains % per 1% NVIDIA moves its way. The profit buys back $EZ.

      ✕ WrongThe pot loses % per 1% NVIDIA moves against. It can be wiped out.
