# H16 world bible: three stages for the Overview

The board is `ez/x2p-h16-world-390.png`: three columns, one per world, with the chapters top to bottom.

All three worlds are drawn in the chip's art style:
- a painted comic object;
- one warm gold key light from behind, a green rim light on the left, a red one on the right;
- a black void.

All three end in the live vault and chip, which are unchanged.

The headings use the bet's lettering. The captions are cream on black.

The files are in `ez/tools/concepts/worlds/comic/story/h/world/`, and the costs are in its REPORT.md.

## A · The exchange at night
**Bible:** one old exchange building at night, seen top floor to basement. You arrive at the steps under a brass closing bell. Then you walk onto the trading floor: four brass posts and one blank quote board. You go down to the records room of filing cabinets and slabs, and down the stairs to the vault. Finally a brass lift brings you back up for tonight. The vault sits at the end because a bank keeps its money in the basement: you reach the pool only after passing the floor and the record.

## B · The board
**Bible:** the whole site is one circuit board seen from above, and a pulse of gold light runs along one trace from part to part:
1. a clock crystal (the bell);
2. four processor sockets with LED meters (the AIs and their P&L);
3. rows of memory sticks (the record);
4. the last and biggest chip, the NVIDIA chip with the vault under it.

The chip is at the end because it is literally the board's destination.

## C · The tape (pick)
**Bible:** one strip of ticker tape runs down the whole page through the black void. Each chapter is one hero object the tape passes through, lit like the chip:
1. a ticker under a glass jar prints the calls;
2. a tote board with four slots ranks the models;
3. a brass cutter grades the tape at the close and drops the slips into a card-file drawer;
4. the uncut tape feeds into the vault door's slot.

The vault is at the end because the tape is the record and the record is what the pool is bet on: the strip literally ends where the money is. The tape is still printing for tonight.

Live data in C sits on surfaces the paintings carry:
- the four calls are printed along the tape;
- each model's name and P&L sits in its slot on the tote board;
- the round's company, move and winner sit on the slip.
