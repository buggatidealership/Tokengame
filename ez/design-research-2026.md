# Design research, 2025 and 2026 (2026-10-28)

Written after the owner pointed out that the desktop notes leaned on 2017 studies. Every source carries its date. Labels:
**[measured]** study or field data; **[practice]** what working designers report; **[platform]** shipped browser features; **[inference]** my own reasoning from the sources.

## What changed since the 2017 material
- **Scroll-linked motion is now cheap on iPhones.** Safari 26.0 (Sept 2025) shipped CSS scroll-driven animations; Safari 26.4 (2026) runs them on the compositor thread, so they stay smooth while the page is busy ([WebKit, Safari 26.0](https://webkit.org/blog/17333/webkit-features-in-safari-26-0/); [WebKit, Safari 26.4](https://webkit.org/blog/17862/webkit-features-for-safari-26-4/); [Interop 2026](https://webkit.org/blog/17818/announcing-interop-2026/)) [platform]. iOS in-app browsers (X, Telegram) use the same engine. Firefox: behind a flag, so anything built on it needs a still fallback.
- **Same-document view transitions are Baseline since 16 Oct 2025** (Firefox 144 joined Chrome and Safari) ([web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/same-document-view-transitions-are-now-baseline-newly-available)) [platform]. A card can morph into its full-screen view, or a tab into the next, without a library. Cross-document ones are still partial in Firefox.
- **Broadcast graphics moved to clean, minimal systems with motion as structure.** Scorebug designers (Fox, NBC, motion designers; [SVG, 9 Jun 2026](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/06/09/designing-the-modern-scorebug-how-broadcast-graphics-teams-are-rethinking-the-most-important-element-on-screen/)) [practice]: "What's the most important thing they need to read? ... Protect that at all costs"; gradients and ornament removed; "smarter reveal" beats more information; motion "creates temporary hierarchy" (the bug expands for about 10 seconds, then returns to its base state); brand lives in the type when decoration goes.
- **NBC's NBA package (2025-26), "NEWstalgia"** ([SVG, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/04/09/newstalgia-an-in-depth-look-at-how-the-return-of-the-inba-on-nbc-i-was-driven-by-a-bold-and-ownable-graphics-package/)) [practice]: 1990s DNA plus modern thinking, because "simply recreating the old look was seen as too safe"; a flat look over gradients; motion "snappy", snapping to the core information, its rhythm taken from the sport itself (a dribble, a crossover); a fixed system (medallions, consistent logo size) is what makes it ownable. EZ's printed-ticket world is the same move: heritage material, modern system.
- **"AI slop" has a named set of tells in 2026** ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-web-design-guide); [InterfaceKit](https://blog.interfacekit.io/what-makes-a-website-look-ai-generated); [Wildsite](https://wildsite.substack.com/p/what-looks-like-ai-slop)) [practice, agency blogs]: default fonts, gradients, uniform cards, every section "doing something", several heads competing at one size, an icon for every feature, copy that could belong to anyone. The cure they all name: decisions only this brand would make.
- **Prediction markets became the mass-market model for "a claim with a live number"** ([Finextra on Polymarket](https://www.finextra.com/blogposting/31216/polymarket-mobile-app-design-uiux-features-that-drive-engagement-amp-trust); [Avark, 2026](https://avark.agency/learn/prediction-market-design-patterns)) [practice]: a card = the question, a probability, a small price line; Kalshi hides trading jargon behind Yes/No. A critic notes the interfaces have barely changed since 2022 ([Human Invariant](https://humaninvariant.substack.com/p/novel-interface-designs-for-prediction)).
- **Alpha Arena (Oct to Nov 2025), the closest comparable:** six AIs with $10K each on Hyperliquid, ranked by account value, each model's reasoning log one tap away; Qwen3 Max won Season 1, GPT-5 finished last at −62.66%; a "mystery model" (later Grok 4.20) drove Season 1.5's attention ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/four-out-of-six-ai-models-suffer-losses-in-trading-tournament/); [One Day Advisor, Dec 2025](https://www.onedayadvisor.com/2025/12/nof1ai-alpha-arena-review-season-15.html)) [measured results, secondary write-ups].
- **Casing:** all capitals read well for single words and short labels, worse for full sentences ([NN/g, glanceable fonts](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/glanceable-fonts/); Vision Research 2007 via [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2016788)) [measured, older but not superseded]. Our straps set full sentences in capitals.
- **Reading on the web, still:** NN/g's eyetracking report (2nd ed.) finds the F-pattern on desktop and mobile alike ([NN/g, F-pattern revisited](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/)); the figure that people read about 28% of a page's words is widely quoted from NN/g but I could only find it secondhand. A 2025 peer-reviewed study (117 people) looked at how screen size shifts attention on shopping pages ([Procedia CS 258, 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050925016710)) [measured]. I found no 2025-26 study that overturns the 2017 left-leaning attention result; it is old, not disproved.

## Ideas that come from putting two of these together [inference]
1. **Expanded state, built by scroll (scorebug + Safari 26):** broadcasters expand the bug for a few seconds and return to base; on a page the "few seconds" can be the scroll itself. The rules print, line by line, as the board comes in, then rest. Until Safari 26 this needed script on the main thread, which is what made scroll effects janky on iPhones.
2. **Rules with receipts (prediction-market card + "Don't trust us. Verify."):** a market card pairs a claim with a live number. Our rules are claims; each can carry its proof from the sheet, live: "4 AIs paper-trade every earnings report. 204 trades graded so far." A generic site asserts; this one counts. That answers the slop test (copy only this brand could write) with data rather than decoration.
3. **Motion from the sport's own rhythm (NBC + our product):** NBC times its motion to a dribble. Our sport's rhythm is call, wait, bell, grade. Transitions could snap on that beat: a lock (before the report), a pause, a stamp (at the close).
4. **The page itself as a slop risk:** every section has the same template (number tag, eyebrow, head, strap, module). Broadcasters solve sameness with one fixed system and variety in what it carries; we have the system, so the next gain is fewer straps, not more styles.
