4. **The philosophy: only falsifiable outcomes count.** LLMs can't reliably tell narrating from doing. They'll say "done" when
   nothing happened, and they don't know they're doing it. [owner] Research backs this: in agent benchmarks, "false success"
   (the agent says it finished, the system says it didn't) is 44 to 76% of all failures, and 13 to 89% depending on the model. [sourced: arXiv 2606.09863]
   So you can't trust what a model *says*. You design a system where the outcome is a hard fact. The market is that fact. [owner]
