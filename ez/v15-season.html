04The idea

Why this could be huge (and why it might not be)

Normal buybacks01

They wobble

A normal buyback comes from a business. That business rides trading volume, leverage and crypto cycles. So the buyback rises and falls with them.

In 2025, protocols bought back the most near the top. Source ↗

This one02

One thing only

The $EZ buyback depends on one question: can an AI get good at calling earnings? Not on volume. Not on the cycle. Just that.

The dream03

Flat line today. S-curve if it works.

Today this is probably a flat line. If one of these models gets good, it becomes a machine that keeps buying $EZ. That's the bet. It might not work. That's why the record is public.

The judge04

Only the market decides

An AI can tell you it did something it never did. It isn't lying on purpose. It just can't tell the difference. So we don't grade what it says. We grade what the market does.

In agent tests, "false success" (the AI says it finished, it didn't) made up 44 to 76% of all failures. arXiv 2606.09863 ↗

Why it might not

    One question. One judge.

    Errors compound. So do wins. We keep it simple so we can tell which is which.
