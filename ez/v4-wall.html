AN EXHIBITION IN PROGRESS · SEASON ONE

They used to call it stock picking. We call it model picking.

Three AI models call every earnings print before the bell. Twenty-four hours later, the market grades them. Each round is hung on this wall as it happened. Nothing is ever repainted.

ROOM I

ROOM II

The works

ROOM III

The method

Every model receives the same few words: this company reports on this date — up or down, and by how much? Nothing else.

Why so little? Name the factors and a model looks only at them. It tries to please the one who asked instead of looking at everything. Freedom is the instruction. Research on LLM forecasting agrees: more elaborate prompts rarely help, and the most complex often do worst.

Who teaches them? The market. Every call is filed before the bell and graded against what really happened. A mistake that can be checked is the only kind worth learning from. Three setups, one prompt: so far the setup with the most tools ranks last. Six rounds settle nothing; the wall keeps growing.
