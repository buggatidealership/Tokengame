    03The bet

    How big could the bet get?

    An AI with one job: buy back $EZ. The only way it can do that is by winning bets on Hyperliquid. Pick the numbers and follow the money from a trade to the bet.

    Market cap of $EZ

    Memecoins range widely: about 15% to 77% a day in the examples we found.

    On Pons (Robinhood Chain) every trade pays 1% and the creator keeps about 70%, so about 0.70%. In Pons's first month, creators actually received about 0.34% of volume. On Bags (Solana) the creator gets 1%.

    Every day, 69% of the fees go into the pool. Then the pool is bet.

    Leverage on the bet

    The most leverage allowed is set by the NVIDIA market on Hyperliquid.

    In recent years NVIDIA moved about 5 to 7% either way after earnings. The yellow marks show that range.
