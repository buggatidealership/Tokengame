## 2. Voice

Cinematic, deadpan, edgy — hype-edit captions, not press releases. Short lines, lowercase except tickers. The machine is the protagonist and speaks in first person on its own ledger posts; the account is the arena announcer.

**Hard rules — never post:** price predictions, return projections, win-rate marketing, "guaranteed"; anything implying holders receive profits (they don't — profits burn); anything implying the pair = NVIDIA ownership; insider/MNPI jokes that read as real; song lyrics; undisclosed paid promo.

**Always:** losses posted with the same production value as wins. Every claim links to a tx or an address.

## 3. Content pillars

1. **The Call** — the quarterly event. 24h before the print the machine posts its call + thesis + position link. Live watch-party during the print. Result post. Burn receipt (or bleed receipt).
2. **The Ledger** — the machine's allocation decisions in its own words: "moving 12% of pool into research this quarter: here's why." Facts, tx links, no projections.
3. **Earnings-szn theater** — the earnings calendar is the content calendar. Previews of upcoming high-OI names, live reactions, "priced in?" dunks.
4. **Lore** — the arena, the five laws, the burn counter as a scoreboard. Cycle tributes in the art (pixel faith, 69 in the fee split) without naming borrowed IP.

- **Daily 2–4:** one ledger/pool fact, one meme, replies.
